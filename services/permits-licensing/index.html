Assistance, not issuance

Jerco provides permit assistance and regulatory coordination. Permits and licenses are issued only by the competent authorities. Approval, requirements, fees, and processing times are determined by each authority and may vary by location and project.

How We Assist

How we assist

A structured workflow that keeps your documents, technical data, and coordination records organized from the first conversation onward.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Initial Consultation

    Discuss your project scope, location, and timeline to identify which permits may be relevant, based on the information you share.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Document Preparation

    Help compile and organize supporting documents, drawings, route data, and technical attachments for the applicable process.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Technical Review

    Review technical data such as routes, KMZ/KML, drawings, and BOQ for completeness and consistency before submission.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Authority Coordination

    Support communication and coordination with the relevant authorities and local stakeholders throughout the process.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Follow-up & Documentation

    Track progress, respond to requests for additional information, and keep records organized for your project files.

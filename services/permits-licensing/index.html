Assistance, not issuance

Jerco provides permit assistance and regulatory coordination. Permits and licenses are issued only by the competent authorities or, for commercial content, by the rights holders. Approval, requirements, fees, and processing times are determined by each issuer and may vary by location and project.

Commercial Licensing

Sports public viewing licenses

Showing live sports such as the Premier League, AFL, or NRL to guests in a bar, restaurant, hotel, villa, or beach club is a commercial public viewing. It requires a commercial license from the rights holder or its authorized distributor. Home or personal subscriptions do not cover commercial screening.

Example competitions & sports

  • Premier League
  • UEFA Champions League
  • AFL
  • NRL
  • Super Rugby
  • Formula 1
  • UFC
  • Cricket
  • and other competitions

Commercial rights, terms, and fees differ by competition, season, and territory, and are set by the rights holder.
